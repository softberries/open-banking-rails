Cash tracking

When enabled, every BLIK ATM withdrawal is mirrored as a top-up in your cash wallet, and both rows are classified as transfers (excluded from spend). You're then expected to log cash spending manually under Cash Transactions.

Existing ATM withdrawals stay as-is until you run the backfill task (cash:backfill_atm_links).

Hidden categories

Transactions in the selected categories are always masked (••• instead of title, merchant, category, and amount). Analytics totals stay accurate. Drill-down into these categories / transactions is blocked - to open one, remove the category from this list.
